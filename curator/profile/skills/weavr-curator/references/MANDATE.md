# Mandate: the owner's thesis

This file is the owner's. It is the thesis the curator serves; the signer
never reads it. **Every SIZE and LIMIT lives in the signer's policy document
and nowhere else**: how many legs, how large a leg, the stable sleeve, the
turnover and cost caps, the cadence, the propose window, the notice. The
curator reads them live with `weavr_curator {verb: "policy"}`, and a number
written here would only be a number the curator believes over the signer
that enforces it. So this page holds words: what the portfolio is for, what
it holds, what it never holds, when it moves and when it does not. Edit it,
date the heading, and the curator reads the version in force.

The worked example below is a template. Replace it with your own thesis;
keep it free of numbers.

---

# <TICKER> mandate (<date>)

## Thesis

<TICKER> is a basket a holder can leave alone for a year. It earns the
staking yield of SOL through liquid staking tokens, carries a standing BTC
position, keeps a stablecoin sleeve as ballast and dry powder, and holds at
most one higher-beta alt. It does not chase narratives, it does not time the
market, and it does not leave Solana.

## What it holds

- SOL and its liquid staking tokens: the core, together the largest
  category.
- BTC through a wrapped BTC pool: a standing position, never the majority.
- Stablecoins: the ballast; the sleeve grows when the owner wants ballast,
  not when the curator is nervous.
- One alt at most, and only with a reason a holder would accept; zero is a
  fine number.

The policy's universe allowlist and categories are the shelf; the policy's
shape rules are the sizes. Neither is repeated here.

## What it never holds

A pool outside the policy's allowlist, a pool without a price feed, a pool
whose risk tier the policy refuses, anything on another chain.

## When the curator moves

A rebalance is a change of thesis or a change of universe, never a reaction
to price: a held pool leaves the universe (inactive, publisher-parked past
the policy's limit, risk tier raised, cap lowered), a pool enters that the
thesis wanted, a trailing drawdown below the policy's floor says the sizing
was wrong, or the monthly review finds the mix no longer matches this page.
Between those, HOLD. An underweight leg is the keeper's to heal through
inflows or a top-up deposit, not a proposal.

## What the curator never does

Propose more often than the policy's cadence allows; propose while a change
is pending; withdraw on its own; touch another portfolio; hold a pool the
policy refuses.

## Review cadence

Daily gate (wakes only on a trigger), universe watch, weekly report, monthly
thesis review on the first review weekday of the month. Every proposal lands
after the portfolio's own notice, inside the owner's day.
